# Product strategy (launch assortment)

The launch set focuses on lightweight, visual, high-margin pieces that convert well in mobile browsing and social-first discovery:

1. Layered necklaces
2. Charm necklaces
3. Coin medallion necklaces
4. Ear cuffs
5. Chunky hoops
6. Dainty rings and ring stacks
7. Anklets
8. Birthstone/charm bracelets
9. Personalized initial pieces
10. Gift-ready sets
11. Hair + jewelry crossover accessories
12. Vacation/festival statement pieces

This mix supports low friction gifting, stackable repeat purchases, and strong AOV via bundles.
