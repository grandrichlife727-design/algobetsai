FAQ

Good questions.

How does the 5-Agent Edge Engine decide a pick? ⌄
Each pick is scored by five components: (1) best-line EV, (2) de-vig consensus fair price, (3) sharp-book weighting, (4) steam move pressure when present, and (5) public fade pressure when skew is meaningful. We publish picks only when the combined score clears our edge threshold.
What exactly do Free users get each day? ⌄
Free includes 3 preview cards per day with matchup context only. Bet details (bet type, side, line, and odds) stay locked on free. Premium and VIP unlock full pick details and real-time tool access.
Where does the odds data come from? ⌄
Live odds come from our multi-book market feed across major sportsbooks. We compare book prices, de-vig consensus probability, and line movement on every scan so stale prices fall out quickly.
What sports and markets are covered right now? ⌄
Current focus is NBA, NFL, NHL, NCAAB, MLB, and EPL across sides, totals, moneylines, and selected props. We do not currently run MMA markets in the app.
What is the difference between Premium and VIP? ⌄
Premium unlocks full picks plus Props Edge, +EV board, Steam, and Fades. VIP includes everything in Premium plus VIP Pick of the Day, live-play tools, line-rush/CLV execution tools, and VIP Discord.
How does the 3-day trial work? ⌄
Premium trial is 3 days with card required at checkout. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid charges. If not canceled, the subscription automatically converts to paid after day 3.
Can I cancel anytime, and are picks guaranteed? ⌄
Yes, you can cancel from billing anytime. Access continues through your paid period. Picks are not guaranteed; AlgoBets Ai is a decision-support product built around pricing edge and long-run discipline, not guaranteed outcomes.
