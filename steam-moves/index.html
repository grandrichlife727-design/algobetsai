Steam Moves: Reading Real Market Pressure

Educational page for bettors who want to use line movement the right way.

Simple definition: A steam move is fast, meaningful line movement across books that suggests strong market pressure.

What steam can tell you

  • Price may be stale at slower books
  • Market is repricing quickly
  • Timing matters as much as side selection

What steam does not guarantee

Steam is signal, not certainty. Late entries at bad prices can erase edge even if the original move was sharp.

How AlgoBets Ai uses steam

The app highlights steam context alongside +EV and consensus inputs so you can avoid chasing and focus on better entries.
